Usage guidelines

Do

  • Use the logos to refer to Selenium in articles, talks, books, and courses.
  • Use the logos to show that your product or service works with Selenium.
  • Leave clear space around the logo, at least the height of the "e" in the mark.
  • Scale the logo proportionally.
  • Pick the green or white version to keep enough contrast with the background.

Don't

  • Change the colors, proportions, or typeface of the logo.
  • Add effects such as shadows, outlines, or gradients.
  • Combine the logo with other logos or use it as part of your own logo.
  • Use the logo in a way that suggests the Selenium project sponsors or endorses your product, service, or event.

The Selenium name and logos are trademarks of the Software Freedom Conservancy, the non-profit home of the Selenium project. If you are unsure whether your use is appropriate, or you need a format not listed here, please reach out.
